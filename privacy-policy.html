Privacy Policy for WrapUp

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

WrapUp ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

WrapUp does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. The app operates entirely on your device and does not require any personal data to function.

Usage Data

  • Timer Settings: All timer configurations, warnings, and settings are stored locally on your device
  • No Analytics: We do not collect usage analytics, crash reports, or any other data about how you use the app
  • No Tracking: We do not use tracking technologies or cookies

In-App Purchases

Payment Processing

  • Google Play Billing: All in-app purchases (tip jar functionality) are processed through Google Play's secure billing system
  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases, which may collect anonymous purchase data for service provision
  • No Payment Data: We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information

Purchase Information

  • Purchase history is managed by Google Play Store
  • We do not store or access your payment details
  • All transactions are processed securely through Google's payment system

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All app data is stored locally on your device
  • Timer settings, configurations, and preferences remain on your device
  • No data is transmitted to external servers

Security

  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect any data stored locally
  • The app does not require internet connectivity for core functionality
  • No sensitive information is collected or transmitted

Third-Party Services

Google Play Services

  • The app uses Google Play Services for in-app purchases
  • Google's privacy policy applies to data collected through their services
  • We do not have access to data collected by Google

RevenueCat

  • RevenueCat is used for managing in-app purchases
  • Their privacy policy applies to data they collect
  • We do not have access to personal data collected by RevenueCat

Children's Privacy

WrapUp does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed to be safe for all ages and does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by:

  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Posting the updated policy in the app or on our website

Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Email: contact@wrapup.app
  • Website: https://wrapup.app

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. All app data is stored locally on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

Consent

By using WrapUp, you consent to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

This privacy policy is effective as of the date listed above and applies to all users of the WrapUp mobile application.
